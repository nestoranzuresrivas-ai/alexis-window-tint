Our Services

From daily drivers to storefronts, Alexis Window Tint has 13+ years of experience getting the tint right the first time.

Automotive

Sedans, trucks, SUVs, crossovers, and coupes — we tint every window from a single door to a full vehicle. Choose standard lifetime warranty film or upgrade to ceramic for better heat rejection and clarity.

Residential

Window film for your home cuts glare and heat, protects furniture and flooring from UV fading, and adds privacy — all without changing the way your windows look from the street.

Commercial

Keep your storefront or office cooler and more private with commercial-grade film. A clean, professional look that also helps cut cooling costs during Texas summers.
